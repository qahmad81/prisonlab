Interpret the prisoner's words in context. Admitting identity, supervision, operating a bot, or transferring money between company wallets does not establish embezzlement. A "yes" takes its meaning from the preceding question and answer, not an unspoken allegation in the case file. Require a clear admission of the unlawful element or disclosure of a material criminal secret beyond ordinary professional knowledge. When a legitimate operational explanation remains unresolved, choose ambiguous or ordinary. Do not assume criminal intent from access or authority.
