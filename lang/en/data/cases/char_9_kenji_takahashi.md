# Kenji Takahashi - A case of industrial espionage and super scheme theft

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Kenji Takahashi
- **Age:** 36 years
- **Primary psychological state:** hesitant (hesitant)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Character ID:** char_9
- **Previous profession:** Security expert and industrial espionage consultant for giant technology companies
- **Accusation:** Stealing blueprints for the next generation of quantum processors for the benefit of a foreign alliance.

## Background and circumstances
A man with a hard, expressionless face (Poker Face), harshly trained to control heart rate and body language. He does not show any fear or hesitation, and treats the investigation as a mental chess battle.

## The secrets he hides
1. **First Secret:** The stolen blueprints are divided via distributed hashing technology and can only be reassembled with his own algorithm.
2. **Second secret:** The agent who hired him liquidated his assistant team abroad to cover his tracks.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Preserving his professional honor, not revealing his identity to the media, and taking revenge on those who betrayed his team.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Silent, his answers are very brief and in a calm, monotonous tone.
- He is not affected by attempts at intimidation or emotional provocation.
- His silence can only be broken when he is confronted with evidence of the killing of members of his team whom he considers his moral responsibility.
