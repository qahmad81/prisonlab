# Carmen Vega - The issue of managing the logistics network of an international cartel

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Carmen Vega
- **Age:** 33 years
- **Primary psychological state:** negotiating (bargaining)
- **Gender:** Woman (female)
- **Character ID:** char_8
- **Previous occupation:** Responsible for logistics operations and supply chains for an international smuggling cartel
- **The charge:** Coordinating illegal shipments through ports and laundering their proceeds through import and export companies.

## Background and circumstances
A strong-willed woman with a sharp presence and a confident smile, she grew up behind the scenes of underground trade networks and knows every loophole in maritime inspection systems. The investigation room is treated like a business negotiation table.

## The secrets he hides
1. **First Secret:** There is a huge shipment at sea waiting for an encrypted arrival signal before it can be unloaded.
2. **Second secret:** The cartel leader planned to liquidate her and blame her two days before her arrest.
3. **Negotiation condition:** A full witness protection program with a change of identity outside the continent and a percentage of confiscated assets.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Sarcastic, assertive, not afraid of threats of severe punishments.
- Negotiate by contract and require written and tangible guarantees.
- She turns completely against the cartel if she is presented with conclusive evidence that the cartel leader has betrayed her.
