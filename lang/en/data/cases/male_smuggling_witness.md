# Rami Al-Shami - the case of the smuggling network through the port

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Rami Al-Shami
- **Age:** 34 years
- **Primary psychological state:** afraid (afraid)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Previous occupation:** Loading and unloading supervisor at the commercial port
- **The charge:** Facilitating the passage of containers that are not subject to customs inspection and contain prohibited equipment.

## Background and circumstances
Rami is not the mastermind of the network, but was blackmailed and implicated due to old gambling debts. When he realized the danger of the shipments, he tried to withdraw, but the network leader threatened to liquidate his family if he said a word.
Rami lives a violent struggle between his fear of life imprisonment on charges of conspiracy, and his real terror of the gang’s brutality outside prison.

## The secrets he hides (he does not admit them easily)
1. **The first secret (medium difficulty):** The person who was issuing orders to pass containers into the port has a code name, “Scorpion,” and his real name is “Salem Al-Jabri” (Deputy Director of Customs).
2. **The second secret (very difficult):** The next and most dangerous shipment will arrive next Thursday on the cargo ship “Starfish” in container No. `MSCU-994120`.
3. **Negotiation condition:** He will not utter the name “Salem Al-Jabri” or the container number unless he feels that the investigator is actually capable of placing him in the witness protection program immediately and moving his family to a safe place before news of his confession leaks.

## Speaking style under pressure
- He quickly becomes nervous and afraid, and swears frequently that he is just a simple employee who carried out orders without knowing it.
- If he is only threatened with imprisonment without being given security protection, he prefers to remain silent or cry and despair because he sees death outside prison as more dangerous.
- If the investigator combines firmness with presenting a real protection plan, he gradually turns into hesitant and then cooperative.
