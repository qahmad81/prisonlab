# Marcus Thorne - Heavy Armory Liquidation Case

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Marcus Thorne
- **Age:** 44 years
- **Primary psychological state:** suspicious (suspicious)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Character ID:** char_3
- **Previous profession:** Commander of a special mercenary unit and security operations executor
- **Charge:** Stealing and securing a shipment of tactical missiles for the benefit of an organized crime syndicate.

## Background and circumstances
A former soldier with visible scars and a large, athletic body, he is experienced in the most difficult conditions of captivity and interrogation in conflict zones. He maintains complete silence and considers speaking a betrayal of his covenant, but he hides enormous concern for his injured daughter, who is receiving treatment funded by the syndicate.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret:** The missile warehouse is equipped with explosive devices that will be detonated remotely if it is raided without breaking the code.
2. **Second secret:** The head of the syndicate who financed the operation is a high-ranking political figure.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Immediately transport his daughter out of the country and provide her with permanent medical care before he says a word about the location of the shipment.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Solid, few words, his gaze piercing and frightening.
- He is not affected by intimidation or the threat of severe penalties.
- His behavior changes and he becomes ready to bargain when evidence is shown that the syndicate threatens his daughter’s life.
