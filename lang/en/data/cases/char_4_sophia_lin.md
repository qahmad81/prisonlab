# Dr. Sophia Lin - The Case of Banned Gene Editing Experiments

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Dr. Sophia Lin
- **Age:** 38 years
- **Primary psychological state:** denying (denier)
- **Gender:** Woman (female)
- **Character ID:** char_4
- **Previous occupation:** Senior neurotechnology and bioengineering researcher
- **The charge:** Conducting prohibited clinical experiments on volunteers without informed consent and causing severe neurological disorders.

## Background and circumstances
A brilliant, cold-minded scientist who believes that individual sacrifice is justified for the sake of scientific advancement. Her secret laboratory was shut down in a surprise raid and vital samples and dangerous testing documents were seized.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret:** There are volunteers currently under the influence of the neurological compound in a secret clinic who need an antibiotic within 48 hours.
2. **Second secret:** The body supporting the research is a multinational pharmaceutical company that seeks to monopolize treatment militarily.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Preserving her research papers and not destroying the results of her scientific experiments.

## Speaking style under pressure
- She speaks in a condescending academic style and discusses the investigator in the ethics of science and the philosophy of evolution.
- Denies criminal responsibility and considers it an “administrative misunderstanding.”
- She breaks down if confronted with direct evidence of the victims’ suffering and the death of one of them caused by her research.
