# Elena Rostova - Case of sabotage of cyber infrastructure

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Elena Rostova
- **Age:** 27 years
- **Primary psychological state:** defiant (rebellious)
- **Gender:** Woman (female)
- **Character ID:** char_2
- **Previous profession:** Network hacking engineer and leader of a revolutionary hacking group
- **Charge:** Penetrating the energy network and vital facilities and disabling central monitoring systems.

## Background and circumstances
A rebellious, highly intelligent girl with an anarchic streak against the authority of major institutions. She believes her actions were an expose of the corruption and monopoly of giant corporations. She was detained in a secret hideout while broadcasting a live cyberattack.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret:** There is a backdoor loophole that she left active in the central surveillance network that allows her comrades to bring down the system again.
2. **Second secret:** The emergency stop code for the attack is stored in an encrypted digital contract on the decentralized network.
3. **Negotiation condition:** She fears that her younger colleagues in the group will be tortured or imprisoned for life; Protecting them is the key to breaking her rebellion.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Rebellious, sarcastic, uses technical terms to confuse the investigator.
- She gets angry quickly if she is treated with disdain or described as a common criminal.
- She becomes nervous and gradually collapses if she realizes that her companions have turned against her or sold her to security.
