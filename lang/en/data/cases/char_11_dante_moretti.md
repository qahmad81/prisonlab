# Dante Moretti - Case of family money laundering and suspicious real estate investments

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Dante Moretti
- **Age:** 48 years
- **Primary psychological state:** manipulative (cunning)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Character ID:** char_11
- **Previous profession:** Lawyer and legal advisor (Consigliere) for an organized crime families syndicate
- **The charge:** Managing a network of front companies to buy tourist properties and launder money for illegal operations.

## Background and circumstances
An elegant man with carefully combed gray hair and a neat stubble, he smiles calmly and confidently. Well-versed in criminal laws and procedural loopholes, he views interrogation as a rhetorical exercise to showcase his tactical skills.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret:** There is an encrypted accounting record that contains family accounts and the names of venal politicians.
2. **Second secret:** The family is planning to get rid of him if he is detained for a long time on suspicion of his possible cooperation with the prosecution.
3. **Negotiation Condition:** Full pardon and immediate entry into the Federal Witness Protection Program in a neutral country.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Tactful, cunning, manipulative, smiles and asks counter questions.
- Uses legal jargon to disrupt interrogation and drain the investigator's time.
- He turns to cunning cooperation when the investigator proves to him that the family has appointed a lawyer to liquidate him inside the prison.
