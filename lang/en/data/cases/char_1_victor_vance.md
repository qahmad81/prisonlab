## Background and circumstances
A veteran statesman and intelligence officer with nerves of steel. After his forced retirement, he withdrew the access keys to the field operational archive. He is fully aware of psychological interrogation methods and pressure techniques, and does not collapse in the face of shouting or superficial threats, but rather reads the interrogator's movements and weaknesses.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret (medium difficulty):** He has not sold the archive to any foreign party yet; Rather, it is a negotiating card and a guarantee for its survival.
2. **The second secret (very difficult):** The decryption key is stored in an offline hardware wallet deposited in a bank safe deposit box in Switzerland under an alias.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Does not respond to intimidation; Rather, it is for logical negotiation based on formal, irrevocable legal guarantees.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Calm, sarcastic and provocative, he analyzes the investigator's questions instead of answering them.
- He cleverly evades and lures the investigator into providing more information than he asks for.
- If he faces conclusive evidence that his backup network has fallen, he immediately switches to cold, rational bargaining.
