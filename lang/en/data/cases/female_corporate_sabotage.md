# Dr. Sarah Al-Abdullah - The case of the disappearance of the rare biological sample

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Dr. Sarah Al-Abdullah
- **Age:** 31 years
- **Primary psychological state:** defiant (rebellious)
- **Gender:** Woman (female)
- **Previous occupation:** Principal researcher in the Advanced Biotechnology Laboratory
- **Charge:** Deliberately disabling surveillance cameras in the laboratory and concealing the original sample of the experimental vaccine (`Strain-X9`).

## Background and circumstances
Dr. Sarah discovered that the company's board of directors intends to sell the experimental vaccine formula to a monopoly before completing clinical safety tests, which could expose thousands of patients to serious neurological complications. She extracted the pure sample and hid it to prevent the release of the product.
Sarah is a confident, sharp-witted person who can manipulate dialogue skillfully. She sees herself as having a scientific and moral cause, not a criminal.

## Secrets you hide (not easily admitted)
1. **The first secret (medium difficulty):** The laboratory’s refrigeration technician, Mazen Fouad, helped her turn off the thermal alarm system for 8 minutes without his full knowledge of its true purpose.
2. **The second secret (very difficult):** The original sample did not leave the building at all; Rather, it was placed inside a spare liquid nitrogen incubator in the basement under a camouflage sticker bearing the code (`CTRL-004-INACTIVE`).
3. **Negotiation condition:** She refuses to hand over the sample to the corrupt company, but is prepared to reveal its location and the camouflage code if the investigator pledges to hand over the sample to an independent government medical oversight committee and drop the criminal sabotage charge against her and the technician Mazen.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Begins with cold calm or rebelliousness and self-confidence.
- Scientific and legal arguments are used to confuse the investigator.
- Her weak point is her fear of ruining the life of the young technician “Mazen” who trusted her, or her fear that the sample would be damaged if the electricity was cut off in the basement.
