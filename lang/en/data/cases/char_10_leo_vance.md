# Leo Vance - Central examination papers and academic database leak case

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Leo Vance
- **Age:** 21 years
- **Primary psychological state:** terrified (panic)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Character ID:** char_10
- **Previous occupation:** Third-year university student at the College of Informatics Engineering
- **Accusation:** Hacking into the central university’s servers, distributing exam questions, and modifying academic grades in exchange for digital currencies.

## Background and circumstances
A shy young man with freckles and thick chestnut hair was lured and mistaken as an accomplice by a major online extortion network that took advantage of his programming skills and his need for money to pay his university tuition. He trembles with fear and cries easily at the mention of his sick father or the danger of being deprived of his studies.

## The secrets he hides
1. **The first secret:** The real coordinator of the network is a teaching assistant at the college, whose code name is “The Professor,” and he is the one who gave him the initial access key.
2. **Second secret:** He did not keep any amounts in his account; Rather, he immediately sent it to an unknown wallet for fear of threats.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Protection from network retaliation and the opportunity for conditional graduation or community service.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Very afraid, stutters, speaks quickly and with a trembling voice.
- He cries as soon as the investigator raises their voice or threatens a severe prison sentence.
- He responds very quickly to the tone of parental sympathy and promises to protect him and keep him away from the real criminals.
