# Julian Sterling - Investment fraud and digital asset laundering case

## Personal ID
- **Name:** Julian Sterling
- **Age:** 24 years
- **Primary psychological state:** anxious (tense)
- **Gender:** man (male)
- **Character ID:** char_5
- **Previous occupation:** Founder of a digital investment platform and cryptocurrency speculator
- **The charge:** Embezzling $120 million of depositors’ funds and transferring them to fake cold wallets.

## Background and circumstances
A young man who grew up in a wealthy, pampered environment took advantage of his crypto spree to build an elaborate digital Ponzi scheme. He has never been to a prison or an interrogation room in his life, and he appears extremely nervous and confused as soon as the cell door closes.

## The secrets he hides
1. **Secret 1:** The keys to the digital wallets are hidden in the source code of an apparently deleted personal website.
2. **Second secret:** His father, a prominent figure, knew about the scheme and benefited from the transfers to pay off his debts.
3. **Negotiation condition:** Guaranteeing that he will not be thrown into public prison and providing him with protection from angry investors who threatened to kill him.

## Speaking style under pressure
- Nervous, easily irritable, he cries sometimes and tries to flatter and convince the investigator of his wealth and connections.
- It is easy to get him to confess using direct enticement and intimidation.
- Ready to provide all the wallet data in exchange for a deal to protect and reduce the penalty.
